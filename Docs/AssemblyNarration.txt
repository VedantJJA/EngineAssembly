Welcome to Engine Lab. We will assemble a three-cylinder engine, one section at a time. Read each instruction and complete its parts before moving on.

BLOCK AND CRANKSHAFT

Place the cylinder block on the workpiece. The three cylinder bores identify the top; the crankshaft opening is underneath.

Turn the block upside down. Fit the two needle bearings and four upper main-bearing shells into their matching crankshaft bearing positions.

Lower the crankshaft into the bearings in the open crankcase. Align its journals with the bearing supports.

PISTONS AND CONNECTING RODS

Start the cylinder 1 piston on its bench fixture. Cylinder 1 is the bore at the timing-drive end. Place the piston with its crown above the wrist-pin bore.

Put the small end of the connecting rod between the piston bosses. Align its bore with the wrist-pin holes.

Slide the wrist pin through the piston and the connecting rod small end.

Fit both wrist-pin circlips into the grooves at opposite ends of the seated wrist pin.

Fit both big-end studs into the connecting rod. Leave the separate rod cap off for now.

Fit the oil-control ring into the lowest ring groove on the piston.

Fit the second compression ring into the middle piston groove.

Fit the top compression ring into the highest piston groove.

Start the cylinder 2 piston on its bench fixture. Cylinder 2 is the bore at the centre of the block. Place the piston with its crown above the wrist-pin bore.

Slide the wrist pin through the piston and the connecting rod small end.

Fit both wrist-pin circlips into the grooves at opposite ends of the seated wrist pin.

Fit both big-end studs into the connecting rod. Leave the separate rod cap off for now.

Fit the oil-control ring into the lowest ring groove on the piston.

Fit the second compression ring into the middle piston groove.

Fit the top compression ring into the highest piston groove.

Start the cylinder 3 piston on its bench fixture. Cylinder 3 is the bore at the flywheel end. Place the piston with its crown above the wrist-pin bore.

Put the small end of the connecting rod between the piston bosses. Align its bore with the wrist-pin holes.

Slide the wrist pin through the piston and the connecting rod small end.

Fit both wrist-pin circlips into the grooves at opposite ends of the seated wrist pin.

Fit both big-end studs into the connecting rod. Leave the separate rod cap off for now.

Fit the oil-control ring into the lowest ring groove on the piston.

Fit the second compression ring into the middle piston groove.

Fit the top compression ring into the highest piston groove.

Keep the engine upright. Install the complete piston units into their bores: C1 at the timing end, C2 in the middle, C3 at the flywheel end. Each open rod big end goes around its crankshaft journal.

Turn the engine upside down. Fit each separate big-end cap around the lower half of its crankshaft journal, against the matching rod.

Fit the two nuts on each connecting-rod cap stud. Complete all three rod caps.

LOWER CRANKCASE AND OIL SUMP

Keep the engine upside down. Fit the four lower main-bearing shells into the lower bearing positions.

Place the lower crankcase bedplate over the bearing supports on the underside of the engine.

Install the bedplate bolts in their matching holes around the lower crankcase.

Start the oil sump on the separate bench fixture, with its open bowl facing upward.

Fit the oil-sump baffle inside the open sump. Align its four screw holes with the mounting points.

Install the four screws securing the baffle inside the sump.

Keep the engine upside down. Pick up the complete sump-and-baffle unit and place it against the underside of the lower crankcase.

Fit the oil-sump fasteners into the matching holes around the installed sump.

Turn the engine upright. Fit the flywheel onto the rear flange of the crankshaft.

Place the flywheel retaining plate over the centre of the flywheel.

Install the six bolts through the flywheel retaining plate.

CYLINDER HEAD AND VALVES

Flip the loose head upside down. Insert all twelve valves from the combustion-chamber side, guiding each stem through its matching guide. There are two intake and two exhaust valves per cylinder.

With the loose head upright, place the spring seat around the valve guide for C1 IN1.

Fit the stem seal over the valve stem for C1 IN1, inside the spring seat.

Place the valve spring around the stem for C1 IN1, resting it on the spring seat.

Place the retainer over the top of the spring for C1 IN1. The model uses its fitted spring position.

Fit both keeper halves around the stem groove for C1 IN1, inside the retainer.

CAMSHAFTS AND TIMING DRIVE

Fit the eight camshaft needle bearings into the supports along the top of the head.

Fit the intake and exhaust camshafts into their respective bearing supports on the head.

Place the front engine cover on the timing-drive end of the engine.

Insert the two pulley keys into the keyways at the ends of the camshafts.

Slide each camshaft pulley hub onto its keyed camshaft end.

Place each camshaft washer inside its pulley hub before fitting the hub bolt or outer timing pulley.

Install the two bolts through the seated washers to secure the camshaft pulley hubs.

Insert the crankshaft pulley key into the crankshaft end keyway.

Fit the outer camshaft timing pulleys onto the secured hubs, and the crankshaft pulley onto its keyed shaft.

Place the crankshaft pulley washer over the crankshaft end, against the pulley.

Install the outer pulley fasteners in their matching holes and on the crankshaft end.

Fit the timing-idler mounting plate beside the timing pulleys.

Install the four screws securing the timing-idler mounting plate.

Fit the two timing-idler shafts into the mounting plate.

Fit the two inner shaft circlips, one on each timing-idler shaft, in the grooves nearest the mounting plate.

Start timing idler 1 on the bench. Place its pulley before inserting the bearings.

Fit both ball bearings into the bore of timing idler 1.

Fit both bore circlips into timing idler 1 to retain its bearings.

Start timing idler 2 on the bench. Place its pulley before inserting the bearings.

Fit both ball bearings into the bore of timing idler 2.

Fit both bore circlips into timing idler 2 to retain its bearings.

Slide both completed timing-idler units onto their matching shafts, against the installed inner shaft circlips.

Fit the two outer shaft circlips into the exposed grooves to retain the installed timing idlers.

Route the timing belt around the crankshaft pulley, both camshaft pulleys and the timing idlers.

HEAD COVER AND FUEL SYSTEM

Place the cylinder-head cover over the camshafts on top of the head.

Install the cylinder-head cover bolts around the cover.

Fit the three fuel injectors into their matching cylinder-head positions.

Place the fuel rail over the tops of the three injectors.

Install the two screws securing the fuel rail.

Connect the fuel return line to its matching fuel-system connection.

Place the crankcase breather housing against its mounting face.

Install the two screws securing the breather housing.

Connect the breather hose between its matching ports.

TURBOCHARGER AND EXHAUST

Fit the combined exhaust manifold and turbocharger housing to the exhaust side of the cylinder head.

Install the four screws securing the exhaust-manifold and turbocharger housing.

Fit the two turbo-shaft bearings into the turbocharger housing.

Slide the turbo rotor shaft through the two bearing positions.

Fit the two circlips into the retaining grooves on the turbo shaft.

Fit the compressor wheel and turbine wheel onto their respective ends of the turbo shaft.

Install the two M4 turbo-wheel fasteners in their matching positions.

Fit the exhaust outlet and catalyst housing to the turbine outlet.

Place the band clamp around the exhaust-outlet joint.

Install the M8 bolt after the turbo outlet clamp is seated.

INTAKE AND EXTERNAL PLUMBING

Fit the intake manifold to the intake side of the cylinder head.

Connect the intake pipe fitting and union to their matching intake connections.

Connect the external service tube along its matching route on the engine.

ACCESSORIES AND MOTOR

Fit the upper accessory mounting bracket to the engine first.

Fit the lower accessory mounting bracket below the upper bracket.

Place accessory spacers A and B into their matching bracket positions.

Mount the alternator and starter motor onto their matching supports.

Fit the alternator pulley onto the alternator shaft.

Fit the accessory-idler shaft into its mounting support.

Fit the inner shaft circlip in the groove nearest the accessory-idler mounting support.

Start the accessory idler on the bench. Place its pulley before inserting the bearings.

Fit both ball bearings into the accessory-idler pulley bore.

Fit the two bore circlips into the accessory idler to retain its bearings.

Slide the complete accessory-idler unit onto the shaft, against the installed inner shaft circlip.

Fit the outer shaft circlip into the exposed groove to retain the accessory idler.

Mount the complete motor unit onto its supports. Its internal components remain one assembly item.

Install both motor mounting bolts through the matching supports.

Fit both motor mounting nuts onto the installed bolts.

Route the accessory drive belt around its matching pulleys to complete the engine.
