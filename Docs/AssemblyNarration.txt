Welcome to Engine Lab. You will build a three-cylinder engine one section at a time. Each instruction first names the part, then tells you where it fits. Read the part label before picking it up. Complete the current group before taking parts for the next group.
The timing-belt end is the end with the belt pulleys. The flywheel end is the opposite end, where the large round flywheel disc fits. Cylinder one is nearest the timing-belt end, cylinder two is in the middle, and cylinder three is nearest the flywheel end.
Some sections are built separately. These are called subassemblies: a piston and connecting rod, for example. Finish the separate unit, then move the whole unit into the engine. You can turn or raise a workpiece for a better view without changing its assembly order.
On the computer, use Left and Right Arrow to choose a lift, and Up and Down Arrow to adjust its height. The white lift is selected. F lowers that workpiece below the floor, turns it, and brings it back. V shows a pointer to the next part or its fitting position. On a phone, choose the next part card and tap its visible target. Next Step demonstrates one installation for you.
This is a learning sequence for the virtual model. No tightening torque, timing setting, or real workshop adjustment is specified.

BLOCK AND CRANKSHAFT

Step 1. Start with the cylinder block, the large body with three round holes. Place it in the main engine work area.

Step 2. Place the smaller needle bearing in its matching round recess on the open crankcase side.

Step 3. Place the larger needle bearing in its matching round recess on the open crankcase side.

Step 4. Fit each curved upper main-bearing shell into a matching crankshaft support. Keep its bearing surface facing the crankshaft.

Step 5. Fit the crankshaft into the open underside of the block. Its smooth round sections rest in the bearings you just placed.

CAMSHAFTS AND TIMING DRIVE

Step 1. Fit the eight camshaft needle bearings into the supports along the top of the cylinder head.

Step 2. Lay the exhaust camshaft in its bearings above the exhaust-valve row.

Step 3. Lay the intake camshaft in its bearings above the intake-valve row.

Step 4. Fit the front engine cover at the timing-belt end of the block. This is the end opposite the flywheel.

Step 5. Fit a small pulley key into the slot at the end of each camshaft. The key keeps the pulley hub from turning on its shaft.

Step 6. Slide each pulley hub onto its camshaft end. Line up the slot inside the hub with the key you just fitted.

Step 7. Place a washer inside each camshaft pulley hub. These washers must go in before the hub bolts or outer pulleys.

Step 8. Fit a bolt through each seated washer to secure the two camshaft pulley hubs.

Step 9. Fit the crankshaft pulley key into the slot at the timing-belt end of the crankshaft.

Step 10. Fit each camshaft timing pulley onto its secured hub.

Step 11. Fit the crankshaft pulley onto the keyed end of the crankshaft.

Step 12. Place the crankshaft pulley washer over the shaft end, against the outer face of the pulley.

Step 13. Fit the outer pulley bolts into their matching camshaft-pulley holes.

Step 14. Fit the large M22 nut onto the crankshaft pulley end.

Step 15. Fit the timing-idler mounting plate beside the timing pulleys. It supports the two small belt-guide pulleys.

Step 16. Fit the four screws through the timing-idler mounting plate.

Step 17. Fit the two idler shafts into the mounting plate. Each shaft will carry one small belt-guide pulley.

Step 18. Fit one inner circlip onto each idler shaft, in the groove closest to the mounting plate. Fit these before the idler units.

Step 19. Start timing idler 1 in its separate work area. Place the small pulley with its centre hole ready for the bearings.

Step 20. Fit both ball bearings into the centre hole of timing idler 1.

Step 21. Fit the two retaining circlips into the grooves inside timing idler 1. They hold its bearings in place.

Step 22. Start timing idler 2 in its separate work area. Place the small pulley with its centre hole ready for the bearings.

Step 23. Fit both ball bearings into the centre hole of timing idler 2.

Step 24. Fit the two retaining circlips into the grooves inside timing idler 2. They hold its bearings in place.

Step 25. Slide each complete timing-idler unit onto its matching shaft, until it reaches the inner circlip already on the shaft.

Step 26. Fit an outer circlip into the exposed groove on each shaft. These clips stop the complete idlers sliding off.

Step 27. Fit the timing belt around the crankshaft pulley, the two camshaft pulleys, and the two timing idlers. The timing belt links the crankshaft and camshafts.
