BONUS CHAPTER: TIMING IDLER BEARING REPAIR

Remove the timing belt to expose the idler. Pick it up, then press G to set it aside.

Remove the outer shaft circlip from timing idler 1. Press G to set it aside; leave the inner shaft clip fitted.

Pull timing idler 1 off its shaft as a complete unit. Press G to put it on the small work platform.

At the small platform, remove the first bore circlip retaining the idler bearings. Press G to set it aside.

Remove the second bore circlip from the idler and press G to set it aside.

Remove the outer idler bearing to reach the faulty inner bearing. Press G to set it aside for reuse.

Remove the faulty inner idler bearing. Press G to discard it from this repair; use the new replacement bearing next.

Pick up the new replacement bearing from the parts table and fit it into the vacant inner bearing seat in the idler.

Refit the original outer bearing into the remaining idler bearing seat.

Refit the first bore circlip to retain the bearings.

Refit the second bore circlip. The idler is complete again.

Pick up the rebuilt timing idler and fit it back onto its shaft, against the retained inner shaft circlip.

Refit the outer shaft circlip to secure the rebuilt idler.

Refit the timing belt around its original pulleys and idlers to complete the repair.
